Welcome to the Pink City

Jaipur is a lively city of pink façades, royal palaces, busy bazaars and warm hospitality. It offers visitors an unforgettable introduction to Rajasthan.

A Planned Royal Capital

Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is known for its carefully planned streets and enduring Rajput architecture.

Famous Places

Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar show the city’s blend of science, art and royal history.

Living Culture

Block printing, folk music, colourful festivals and delicious dal baati churma keep Jaipur’s traditions vibrant today.
